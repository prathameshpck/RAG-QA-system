Steps to follow when building application 
    Request spot instance and SSH into it using you pinto.pem key 
    Attach an EBS storage to it (free tier comes with 30gb)
    Change your docker daemon to store all your docker files into a directory in your EBS. 
    This will allow you to have plenty of space to load your LLM container and setup your VLLM server
